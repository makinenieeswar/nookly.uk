Why Nookly

Designed by someone who has worked the shifts

Every feature exists because a frontline care professional identified a real problem it solves. Nothing is added for show.

AI-assisted, human-approved

AI structures documentation from plain carer notes. Every entry is reviewed and signed off by staff before it becomes part of the record — speed without sacrificing safety.

Proactive risk detection

Nookly's flagship feature identifies emerging health risks and deterioration patterns in real time — enabling early intervention before a situation becomes a crisis.

Family portal included

Families see approved daily updates, care activities, wellbeing summaries, and appointments in real time. Genuine transparency included in every plan — not an extra.

Automated shift handovers

Affordable, fixed pricing

Mobile-first for carers

How it works

From carer note to manager insight in seconds

Four steps that turn everyday care interactions into structured records, risk alerts, and compliant documentation — automatically.

1

Carer records the visit

Using guided templates and dropdowns on the mobile app — quick, structured, minimal free-text required. Available on iOS and Android.

2

AI structures the notes

Nookly's AI organises plain entries into medication updates, wellbeing logs, health concerns, and visit summaries — instantly and accurately.

3

Staff review & approve

No AI-generated content reaches the record without human review and explicit staff sign-off. Safety is built into every single workflow step.

4

Managers see it instantly

Risk alerts, compliance dashboards, handover reports — all generated from approved records in real time, visible on any device anywhere.

Founder story
"Documentation is the key, and care is the lock."

My name is Swathi Silarapu. I founded Nookly after three and a half years as a Mental Health Support Worker supporting individuals with mental health conditions, learning disabilities, autism, and complex support needs across the UK.

My MSc research in Biotechnology focused on the early detection of Chronic Kidney Disease — work that taught me how data, observation, and timely intervention change outcomes. When I moved into frontline care, I saw the same principle being undermined every day: carers spending hours on paperwork when that time could be spent with the people they support.

Nookly's proactive risk detection feature came directly from that research background — identifying subtle signals in daily records before they become clinical emergencies. My vision is to transform care documentation from a compliance burden into a tool that genuinely improves lives.
